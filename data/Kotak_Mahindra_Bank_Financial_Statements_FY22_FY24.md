## 5. Narrative Disclosures & Red-Flag Audit Trails
*Contextual notes for Trend & Red Flag Agent and Retrieval/Grounding Agent*

1. **Industry-Leading Margins & Return Metrics**:
   - *Observation*: NIM stood at 5.28% and RoA reached 2.51% in FY24, positioning Kotak as the highest-margin private bank among large Indian lenders.
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: High CASA sticky base, conservative risk selection, and yield enhancement from SME, microfinance (BSS Microfinance), and consumer finance verticals.
2. **CASA Run-down Pattern (60.75% -> 52.88% -> 45.56%)**:
   - *Observation*: Kotak experienced the sharpest CASA percentage reduction in its peer group (contracting by ~1,519 bps over two years).
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: Rising term deposit rates caused high-net-worth retail and savings depositors to actively reallocate funds into 7.2%-7.4% fixed deposit tenors. Term deposits surged from ₹122,344 Cr in FY22 to ₹244,400 Cr in FY24.
3. **Regulatory Operational Risk Flag (RBI Restriction Context)**:
   - *Observation*: In late FY24 / early FY25, RBI imposed temporary bans on digital customer onboarding and credit card issuance.
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: Elevated IT audit scrutiny surrounding disaster recovery redundancy, data security governance, and capacity management. While capital adequacy remains fortress-level (CAR > 20.5%), customer acquisition cost (CAC) and fee momentum face near-term constraints.
4. **Pristine Asset Quality**:
   - *Observation*: Net NPA contracted to 0.34% with credit costs at historic lows.
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: Cautious corporate loan underwriting, high collateral coverage, and low restructuring pool.
