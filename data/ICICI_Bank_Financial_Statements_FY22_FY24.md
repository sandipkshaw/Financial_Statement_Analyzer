## 5. Narrative Disclosures & Red-Flag Audit Trails
*Contextual notes for Trend & Red Flag Agent and Retrieval/Grounding Agent*

1. **Accrual-Quality Analysis (FY23 Divergence & FY24 Rebound)**:
   - *Observation*: Net Operating Cash Flow dipped to -₹2,094.30 Cr in FY23 before accelerating to ₹91,250.60 Cr in FY24.
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: Temporary mismatch in FY23 loan disbursements (domestic loan growth of +20.5%) relative to deposit mobilization timing. In FY24, operating cash flow conversion was exceptionally strong, exceeding PAT by 2.6x as deposits expanded by ₹231,984.00 Cr.
2. **Superior Pricing Power & NIM Expansion**:
   - *Observation*: NIM expanded from 3.96% in FY22 to 4.53% in FY24, bucking industry-wide margin compression trends.
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: High proportion of repo-linked floating rate loans (~50% of the loan book), disciplined retail deposit repricing, and a favorable mix of high-margin retail loans and business banking advances.
3. **Credit Provision Moderation & Asset Quality**:
   - *Observation*: Credit provisions dropped drastically from ₹8,641.40 Cr (FY22) to ₹3,640.20 Cr (FY24), with Net NPA reaching 0.42%.
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: Proactive contingency provisioning during FY21-FY22 enabled substantial write-backs and exceptionally low incremental credit costs (32 bps in FY24).
4. **CASA Dilution vs Term Deposit Accretion**:
   - *Observation*: CASA ratio contracted from 49.12% in FY22 to 43.15% in FY24.
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: Systemic liquidity absorption led depositors to allocate funds into higher-rate term deposits (+29.5% YoY in FY24).
