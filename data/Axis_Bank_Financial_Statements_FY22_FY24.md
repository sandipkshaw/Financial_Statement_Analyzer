# Financial Statement & Grounding Dossier: Axis Bank Limited

## Metadata & Entity Overview
- **Entity Legal Name**: Axis Bank Limited
- **Ticker / Identifiers**: NSE: AXISBANK | BSE: 532215 | ISIN: INE238A01034
- **Sector / Industry**: Banking & Financial Services / Private Sector Commercial Bank
- **Reporting Currency**: Indian Rupee (INR) in Crores (`₹ in Crores`)
- **Fiscal Year Definition**: April 1 to March 31
- **Periods Covered**: FY 2021-22 (FY22), FY 2022-23 (FY23), FY 2023-24 (FY24)
- **Accounting Framework**: Banking Regulation Act, 1949, RBI Regulations, and Indian GAAP
- **Key Corporate Event Note**: On March 1, 2023, Axis Bank completed the acquisition of Citibank India's consumer business (credit cards, retail loans, wealth management, and retail deposits). FY23 results include an exceptional one-off charge of ₹12,489.82 Cr (goodwill/amortization and integration costs) which led to a reported accounting net loss in Q4 FY23 and muted full-year FY23 PAT. FY24 represents the first normalized post-acquisition year.

## 5. Narrative Disclosures & Red-Flag Audit Trails
*Contextual notes for Trend & Red Flag Agent and Retrieval/Grounding Agent*

1. **Exceptional Items & Accrual Divergence (FY23 Citibank Deal Impact)**:
   - *Observation*: FY23 RoE plummeted to 7.95% and CFO dropped to -₹6,020.72 Cr.
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: Integration of Citibank India portfolio required an upfront accounting write-off of ₹12,489.82 Cr (reflected as an exceptional line item EXC_01). In FY24, earnings normalized with PAT leaping +93.2% YoY to ₹18,512.40 Cr and CFO turning positive to ₹30,682.60 Cr.
2. **Elevated Cost-to-Income Ratio Trend (47.80% -> 53.31% -> 57.52%)**:
   - *Observation*: Cost-to-income increased significantly over the three-year window.
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: Technology platform migration, harmonization of employee compensation structures post-acquisition, card network changeover, and branch infrastructure investments.
3. **Asset Quality Cleansing**:
   - *Observation*: Gross NPA ratio improved from 2.82% to 1.43%, and Net NPA reached 0.31% by March 31, 2024.
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: Rigorous recoveries in corporate book and disciplined underwriting in retail unsecured personal loans and credit cards.
4. **Capital Adequacy & Tier-1 Consumption**:
   - *Observation*: Tier-1 capital ratio declined from 16.34% in FY22 to 13.74% in FY24.
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: Cash payout for the acquisition goodwill and higher risk weightings mandated by RBI on unsecured consumer credit.
