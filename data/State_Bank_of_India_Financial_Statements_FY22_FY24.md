## 5. Narrative Disclosures & Red-Flag Audit Trails
*Contextual notes for Trend & Red Flag Agent and Retrieval/Grounding Agent*

1. **Accrual-Quality Gap (FY23 Divergence)**:
   - *Observation*: Operating Cash Flow (CFO) turned negative to -₹4,064.00 Cr in FY23 despite record-breaking PAT growth (+58.6% to ₹50,237.78 Cr).
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: Aggressive credit expansion (+17.0% YoY in gross advances) with net advance deployment of ₹478,540.20 Cr outpacing deposit mobilization. This gap rebounded in FY24 (CFO recovered to ₹71,422.62 Cr) as term deposit inflows normalized.
2. **Wage Revision & Pension Provision Stress (Cost-to-Income Climbing to 55.56%)**:
   - *Observation*: Operating expenses escalated from ₹85,341.98 Cr in FY22 to ₹114,242.40 Cr in FY24, pushing Cost-to-Income to 55.56%.
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: Implementation of the 12th Bipartite Wage Settlement agreement across public sector bank unions, necessitating one-time and retrospective provisions for wage arrears, pension liabilities, and ex-gratia payments.
3. **Asset Quality Turnaround (Absence of Credit Red Flags)**:
   - *Observation*: Gross NPA dropped from 3.97% to 2.24% and Net NPA compressed to 0.57% in FY24.
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: Structural corporate deleveraging, aggressive resolution in National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) accounts, lower incremental slippages (<1.0%), and sustained write-offs of fully provided vintage legacy NPAs.
4. **CASA Run-off & Liquidity Pressure**:
   - *Observation*: CASA ratio declined steadily from 44.51% to 41.11%.
   - *Audit Trail / Cause*: Depositor rate-sensitivity resulting in migration of institutional and retail savings balances into higher-yielding special retail deposit schemes (e.g., Amrit Kalash).
